Mike Dabydeen helps engineering managers modernize how their teams deliver software in an AI-first era without losing quality, reliability, or team trust.

He brings a rare combination of perspective: former executive leadership as VP Engineering and CTO, active day-to-day software management, and long-running work as an educator and coach.

## How I Help Engineering Managers Adopt AI Responsibly

- Translate AI ambition into a practical operating plan tied to delivery outcomes.
- Build manager enablement systems so adoption is repeatable, not hero-driven.
- Establish decision and governance rhythms that protect quality while accelerating execution.

## Operating Principles for AI-Era Engineering Leadership

- Prioritize business and delivery outcomes over tool experimentation.
- Make tradeoffs explicit with decision logs, guardrails, and review checkpoints.
- Coach managers and developers through capability-building, not one-time training.
- Keep AI adoption practical, measurable, and aligned with engineering standards.

## Engagement Focus

Most engagements are manager-centered workshops and advisory sessions for organizations that need to move from AI exploration to durable execution.
