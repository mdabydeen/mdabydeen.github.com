Developers grow faster when architecture conversations focus on constraints and tradeoffs.

## Coaching Pattern

1. Ask for constraints first.
2. Ask for two viable design options.
3. Compare operational costs, failure modes, and team impact.
4. Decide, then document assumptions.

## Common Failure Mode

Teams debate patterns without naming the problem they are solving. This creates circular conversation and weak decisions.

## Practical Exercise

Use a 30-minute architecture review with a fixed format: five minutes context, ten minutes options, ten minutes tradeoffs, five minutes decision and follow-up tasks.
