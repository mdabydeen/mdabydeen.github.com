The jump between executive leadership and team-level management is not a step down or up. It is a context switch.

## What Changes

1. Time horizon: strategic horizon vs sprint-level execution.
2. Communication: broad narratives vs direct implementation guidance.
3. Metrics: portfolio outcomes vs team throughput and quality.

## What Stays the Same

Strong leaders in both contexts create clarity, remove blockers, and build trust through consistency.

## Practical Tip

Run a weekly reflection on where your time went: strategy, people coaching, delivery operations, and technical depth. Rebalance based on current team needs.
