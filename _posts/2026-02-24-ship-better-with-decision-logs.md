Teams slow down when decisions are made verbally and never captured. A lightweight decision log creates a durable record of context, alternatives, and next steps.

## Practical Template

For each meaningful decision, capture:

1. Context: what changed and why this decision is needed.
2. Options considered: at least two alternatives.
3. Decision: what was selected.
4. Consequences: what this unlocks and what it costs.
5. Review date: when the decision should be revisited.

## Why This Works

Decision logs shorten onboarding time, improve architecture reviews, and prevent repeated meetings about settled topics.

## How to Start This Week

Add a `decisions/` folder in your main repo and commit one short file per decision. Keep the format simple, then evolve it after two sprint cycles.
