Engineering managers do not need another AI tool list. They need a working operating plan that improves execution without increasing delivery risk.

## 90-Day Operating Arc

### Days 1-30: Scope and Baseline

1. Define two to three high-value workflow opportunities.
2. Set baseline metrics for speed, quality, and reliability.
3. Document constraints: compliance, security, platform limits, and team readiness.

### Days 31-60: Pilot with Guardrails

1. Run focused pilots in controlled workflows.
2. Add guardrails for review quality, testing, and human accountability.
3. Capture decision logs on what worked, what failed, and why.

### Days 61-90: Operationalize

1. Standardize successful patterns into team playbooks.
2. Train managers to coach adoption, not just enforce usage.
3. Set monthly review cadence with clear ownership and next-step decisions.

## Manager Scorecard

Use four signals to evaluate progress:

- Delivery impact: cycle-time and throughput improvements.
- Quality impact: defect trends and rollback incidents.
- Adoption health: consistency of usage across teams.
- Decision quality: clarity of tradeoffs and follow-through.

## Common Failure Modes

- Running too many pilots with no prioritization model.
- Treating AI adoption as an individual contributor side project.
- Ignoring change management for managers and tech leads.
- Measuring activity instead of outcome.

## Implementation Prompt

At your next leadership sync, ask: "Which one workflow can we improve in 30 days with clear guardrails and a measurable outcome?" Then commit to a single pilot and review it weekly.
